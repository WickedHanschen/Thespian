Thespian: the emote soundboard for your roleplay

Hit a button, play a beat.

Thespian turns your favourite roleplay lines and emotes into big, clickable buttons, like a soundboard for your character. Stop retyping /me strokes their chin thoughtfully. for the hundredth time: click once and stay in the scene.

What you get

  • 70+ ready-made emotes across five tabs: Greetings, Gestures, Emotions, Actions and Speech
  • Your own buttons: build custom emotes with a label, a channel (Emote /me, Say, Yell, Party or a standard emote like WAVE) and a category
  • Target-aware: write %t and Thespian swaps in your target's name
  • Live preview of the exact chat line, in the builder and when hovering any button
  • Quick emote box for the line that doesn't deserve its own button
  • Minimap button with a golden theatre mask (left-click to open, right-click for options, drag to move)
  • Options panel in the game's own settings: scale, opacity, lock position, close-after-use, tooltips and more
  • Blizzard-style look that fits right into the game
  • No dependencies, no clutter: one small addon, and it never speaks unless you click

How it works

  1. Type /thespian or click the minimap mask.
  2. Pick a tab, click a button. You've emoted.
  3. Want something personal? Click + New Emote, write it, save it. Your buttons show up in green; right-click to edit or delete.

FAQ

Does it send anything on its own? No. Only when you click a button or press Enter.

Where are my custom emotes saved? Account-wide, so all your characters share them.

Does it replace Total RP 3 or other profile addons? No, they do different jobs.

Can I share my emotes? Not yet. Import/export is on the wishlist.
